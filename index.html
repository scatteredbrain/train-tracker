⚠️ Freight Trains (CSX / Norfolk Southern)

Real-time freight train positions are not publicly available. CSX and Norfolk Southern do not expose tracking APIs.

  • Enable Rail Lines overlay (button on map) to see freight corridors
  • Norfolk Southern's Trenton Line and CSX's Trenton Subdivision both cross parts of Bucks County
  • Scanner feeds (e.g. Railstream.net) can give advance audio notice
  • Community reports: Railroading On The Railroad
